## Proven Design Decisions

- Use paper texture, postcards, sticky notes, pencil paths, tape, and restrained hand-drawn accents.
- Keep official evidence compact like a source excerpt, not a full-screen promotional image.
- Use distinct layouts for framework, evidence, questions, worked example, and conclusion.
- Keep titles modest; reserve space for explanatory objects and readable captions.
- Avoid persistent decorative motion, repeated transition effects, edge collisions, and card overlap.

## Proven Audio Decisions

- Keep `voiceover.wav` at normal speed and volume 1.
- Use the selected acoustic-paper BGM as a low bed and duck it around action cues.
- Trigger SFX from visible actions. Pencil, paper, switch, whoosh, and ding each have a distinct semantic role.
- The accepted version starts its BGM directly; do not generalize that preference to every new project.
